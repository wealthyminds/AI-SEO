⚡ Today's Action Plan

5 Quick Wins You Can Do Right Now

Highest impact-to-effort ratio. Do these before anything else.

  • 1
    Allow AI bots in your robots.txt
    Many sites accidentally block GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot. If they can't crawl you, they can't cite you. Fix this in under 5 minutes. See the exact rules below →
  • 2
    Add FAQ Schema to your top 5 pages
    FAQ schema is the single most effective type for Google AI Overview inclusion. Use our Schema Generator to create it in 2 minutes.
  • 3
    Create your llms.txt file
    llms.txt is a business card for AI systems. Use our llms.txt Builder and deploy it to your domain root in minutes.
  • 4
    Add a 50-word direct answer to every key page
    Google AI Overview pulls from the most concise, direct answer on the page. Rewrite your intro paragraph as a crisp 40-60 word answer to the page's main question.
  • 5
    Add Organization schema with sameAs to your homepage
    Organization schema linking to all your social profiles tells Google exactly who you are as an entity — foundational for the Knowledge Panel. Generate it free in our Schema Generator.
