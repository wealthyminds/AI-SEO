⚡ Today's Action Plan

5 Quick Wins You Can Do Right Now

These five actions have the highest impact-to-effort ratio. Do them first, before anything else.

  • 1
    Allow AI bots in your robots.txt
    Many sites accidentally block GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot — the crawlers used by major LLMs. If they can't crawl you, they can't cite you. Check and fix this in under 5 minutes. See the exact rules below →
  • 2
    Add FAQ Schema to your top 5 pages
    FAQ schema is the single most effective schema type for Google AI Overview inclusion. Use our Schema Generator to create it in 2 minutes and paste it into your page's <head>.
  • 3
    Create your llms.txt file
    llms.txt is like a business card for AI systems. It tells LLMs exactly who you are, what you offer, and what pages are most important. Use our llms.txt Builder and deploy it to your domain root in minutes.
  • 4
    Add a 50-word direct answer to every key page
    Google AI Overview pulls from the most concise, direct answer on the page. Rewrite your intro paragraph as a crisp 40-60 word answer to the main question that page covers. This alone can dramatically increase AI Overview citations.
  • 5
    Add Organization schema with sameAs to your homepage
    Organization schema with sameAs links to all your social profiles, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, and Wikipedia (if you have one) tells Google exactly who you are as an entity. This is foundational for Knowledge Panel and AI accuracy. Generate it free in our Schema Generator.
