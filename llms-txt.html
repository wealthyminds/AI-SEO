llms.txt Builder

llms.txt is the emerging standard for telling AI systems exactly who you are, what you do, and what they should know about your brand. Fill in the form and deploy the generated file to your domain root.

💡
What is llms.txt? Proposed by Jeremy Howard, llms.txt is a plain-text file placed at yourdomain.com/llms.txt. It provides LLMs with a structured, concise overview of your site — who you are, your key pages, your permissions, and anything AI systems should know about you. Think of it as a README for AI.
🏢 Brand Identity
Basic information about who you are. This is the "above the fold" section of your llms.txt — every AI system reads this part.
📄 Key Pages
List your most important pages with a brief description of each. AI systems use this to understand your site structure and answer queries about your offerings.
Add your 5-10 most important pages: homepage, services, about, contact, blog, pricing, case studies, FAQ.
🧠 What AI Should Know About You
Key facts, unique selling points, and important context you want AI systems to accurately represent about your brand.
⚙️ Products & Services
List your core offerings so AI systems can accurately recommend you for specific service-related queries.
🔗 Social Profiles & External Presence
Links to your verified profiles. AI systems use these as entity anchors to cross-reference information about you.
🔐 AI Usage Permissions
Specify what AI systems are allowed to do with your content. This helps LLMs use your content responsibly and builds trust.

🚀 How to Deploy Your llms.txt

        
    
          
  1. 
            
    
              Download the file using the button above. The filename must be exactly llms.txt.
            
    
          
    2. 
          
  2. 
            
    
              Upload to your domain root. The file must be accessible at yourdomain.com/llms.txt — not in a subfolder. This is equivalent to where you put robots.txt.
            
    
          
    3. 
          
  3. 
            
    
              Verify it's live by visiting yourdomain.com/llms.txt in your browser. You should see the plain text file.
            
    
          
    4. 
          
  4. 
            
    
              Keep it updated. Update your llms.txt whenever your key pages, services, or brand information changes significantly.
            
    
          
    5. 
          
  5. 
            
    
              Optional: Also create llms-full.txt with expanded documentation, detailed service descriptions, and full content excerpts. Link to it from your llms.txt with: # Full version: /llms-full.txt

📊 What to Expect

        

          
llms.txt is an emerging standard (as of 2025). Not all LLMs currently crawl it, but adoption is growing. Early adopters gain an advantage as AI systems incorporate this signal.

          
Currently known to crawl llms.txt: Perplexity AI, some versions of Bing/Copilot crawlers, and various AI research tools.

          
The broader benefit: Even before widespread LLM crawling, having a well-structured llms.txt demonstrates AI literacy to clients, establishes you as an early adopter, and positions your brand as forward-thinking in the AI era.
